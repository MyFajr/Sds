# My Daily Routine
<small>Everyday habits · Present Simple · General American</small>

> [!abstract] Lesson overview
> Демонстрационный урок для тестирования всех семантических классов ESDS, таблиц, произношения и Callout в Light/Dark.

<span class="st-le">A daily routine is the usual order of things you do every day.</span>

> [!tip] How to use this lesson
> Читай примеры вслух. Сравнивай цвет английских слов, переводов, произношения и грамматических окончаний.

## 3. Grammar — Present Simple

We use the <span class="st-te">Present Simple</span> for habits, routines and general facts.

<span class="st-mi">Use the base verb with I / you / we / they.</span>
<span class="st-im">Add -s / -es with he / she / it in affirmative sentences.</span>

| Subject | Verb form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="st-fo">I / you / we / they</span> | <span class="st-ac">work</span> | <span class="st-ex">I work every day.</span> |
| <span class="st-fo">he / she / it</span> | <span class="st-ac">works</span> | <span class="st-ex">She works every day.</span> |

- I <span class="st-ex">get up</span> at seven.
- She <span class="st-ex">get<span class="st-ac">s</span> up</span> at seven.
- They <span class="st-ex">study</span> English.
- He <span class="st-ex">stud<span class="st-ac">ies</span></span> English.

> [!danger] Common mistake
> <span class="st-wa">❌ She get up at seven.</span>
>
> <span class="st-fa">✅ She gets up at seven.</span>

Use **don’t / doesn’t** to make negatives. Use **do / does** to ask questions.

| Subject | Negative | Example |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="st-fo">I / you / we / they</span> | <span class="st-ac">don’t + verb</span> | <span class="st-ex">I don’t work on Sunday.</span> |
| <span class="st-fo">he / she / it</span> | <span class="st-ac">doesn’t + verb</span> | <span class="st-ex">He doesn’t work on Sunday.</span> |

<span class="st-wa">After doesn’t, do not add -s to the main verb: “She doesn’t work”, not “She doesn’t works”.</span>

> [!bug] Spot the error
> ❌ He doesn’t <span class="st-wa">likes</span> coffee.
>
> ✅ He doesn’t <span class="st-fa">like</span> coffee.

- <span class="st-qu">Do you study English every day?</span>
- <span class="st-qu">Does he work in the morning?</span>
- <span class="st-qu">What time do you get up?</span>

> [!question]+ Pause and answer aloud
> <span class="st-qu">What time do you usually wake up?</span>

## 4. Reading and dialogue

> [!example] Alex’s typical day
> Alex is a student. He <span class="st-ac">wakes up</span> at 7:00. He gets up, brushes his teeth and takes a shower. He usually has breakfast at 7:30. At 8:00, he goes to class. In the evening, he sometimes watches a film. He goes to bed at 11:00.

**Mia:** <span class="st-qu">What time do you get up?</span>

**Ben:** I <span class="st-ac">usually</span> get up at seven. How about you?

**Mia:** Do you have breakfast at home?

**Ben:** Yes, I do.

> [!quote] Useful phrase
> “How about you?” — <span class="st-tr">А ты? / А как насчёт тебя?</span>

## 6. Callout gallery — ESDS palette test

Это словарь Callout-блоков. Сравнивай заголовок, фон, рамку, иконку, контраст текста и вложенные выделения. `+` — раскрытый блок; `-` — свёрнутый. Используется штатный синтаксис Obsidian, не HTML details.

> [!note] Note — заметка
> <span class="st-mi">Базовый информационный блок.</span> Спокойное пояснение.

> [!abstract] Abstract — обзор
> Краткое введение или сводка.

> [!info] Info — информация
> Дополнительный контекст или справочные сведения.

> [!todo] Todo — задача
> Повторить, произнести вслух и применить выражение.

> [!tip] Tip — совет
> <span class="st-ac">Замечай грамматический шаблон целиком.</span>

> [!success] Success — верно
> <span class="st-fa">She gets up at seven.</span> — правильная форма.

> [!question] Question — вопрос
> <span class="st-qu">What do you usually do in the evening?</span>

> [!warning] Warning — предупреждение
> <span class="st-wa">Не добавляй -s после doesn’t.</span>

> [!failure] Failure — ошибка
> ❌ He don’t work here.

> [!danger] Danger — критическая ошибка
> <span class="st-wa">She doesn’t works.</span> — так говорить нельзя.

> [!bug] Bug — типичная проблема
> После “doesn’t” нужен базовый глагол “work”, а не “works”.

> [!example] Example — пример
> **I work.** → **She works.** → **She doesn’t work.** → **Does she work?**

> [!quote] Quote — цитата
> “Practice makes progress.” — <span class="st-tr">Практика помогает двигаться вперёд.</span>

> [!cite] Cite — источник
> Примеры составлены для этой демонстрационной заметки.

> [!faq]- FAQ — сворачиваемый блок
> **Why do we say “she works”?** Because he / she / it takes -s or -es in affirmative sentences.

> [!tip]+ Expanded tip — раскрытый блок
> Нажми на заголовок, чтобы свернуть блок. <span class="st-ac">Вложенный акцент</span> и пример <span class="st-ex">She studies.</span>

> [!example]- Hidden example — свёрнутый блок
> Изначально этот текст скрыт. <span class="st-pr">/ˈstʌdi/</span>

> [!warning]+ Nested emphasis — вложенные акценты
> Обычный текст. <span class="st-wa">Предупреждение</span>, <span class="st-pr">/θ/</span> IPA, <span class="st-fo">think</span> слово.
